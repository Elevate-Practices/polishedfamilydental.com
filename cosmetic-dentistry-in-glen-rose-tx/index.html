What it is

Cosmetic dentistry at Polished Family Dental in Glen Rose

Cosmetic dentistry improves how your teeth look. Bonding uses tooth-colored resin to fix chips and gaps. Veneers are thin porcelain shells that cover the front of teeth to change their shape and color. We'll talk through what you'd like to change and the options that fit.

Questions

Common questions

What's the difference between bonding and veneers?

Bonding is done in one visit with resin and costs less. Veneers are porcelain, made in a lab, and resist stains better.

Is cosmetic dentistry covered by insurance?

Usually not, since it's elective. Membership plan members save 20% on cosmetic work, and Cherry financing can spread the cost.

Can I whiten before getting veneers?

Yes, and it's a good idea, so we can match your veneers to your brighter shade.
