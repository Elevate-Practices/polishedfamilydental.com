Questions

Common questions

Is the membership plan insurance?

No. It's an in-office savings plan. You pay one yearly fee, and your preventive care is covered at our offices.

Can I use it at both offices?

Yes. Membership works at our Fort Worth and Glen Rose offices.

Who can join?

Anyone without dental insurance. The plan is valid in the absence of gum disease; we'll check at your first visit.

What does the Family plan cover?

Up to six people, with two cleanings each, exams, X-rays and free whitening, plus 20% off restorative work. Extra members are $95 each.
