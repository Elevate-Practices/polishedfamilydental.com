Questions

Common questions

Is whitening really free?

Yes. Whitening is included with the Polished Membership Plan and with our $99 new patient special. Ask us for the details.

Does whitening work on crowns or fillings?

No. Whitening only lightens natural teeth, so we plan around any crowns or fillings in your smile.

Will whitening make my teeth sensitive?

Some people feel mild sensitivity for a day or two. We can adjust the treatment if you're prone to it.
