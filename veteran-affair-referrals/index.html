For veterans

VA dental care in Fort Worth & Glen Rose

We're an approved VA dental provider and accept VA referrals at both offices. We can schedule you once an active referral is on file.

Book online Find an office

How it works

Three steps to your VA dental visit.

  1. 1

    Get a referral

    Ask your VA provider or case manager for a community care dental referral to Polished Family Dental in Fort Worth or Glen Rose.

  2. 2

    Confirm it's on file

    Call the office you chose. We can only schedule once we have an active VA referral on file, so we'll check that it has arrived.

  3. 3

    Book and get care

    Once your referral is active, we'll book your visit. Cleanings, fillings, crowns, dentures and implants are all under one roof.

Your part in the referral

The VA issues and approves referrals, so you are part of the process too. If your referral is delayed, expires, or your treatment needs a new referral or approval, please follow up directly with the VA. A call from you is often the quickest way to move it along. We can't schedule your visit until an active VA referral is on file with us, and we'll let you know if anything else is needed from the VA.

Questions

Common questions

Is Polished Family Dental an approved VA dental provider?

Yes. We accept VA referrals at our Fort Worth and Glen Rose offices.

How do I get a VA dental referral?

Ask your VA provider or case manager for a community care dental referral to Polished Family Dental, then call us or book online.

What services can I get with a VA referral?

We provide cleanings, fillings, crowns, dentures and implants. Your VA case manager confirms what your referral covers.

Can I book before my referral is approved?

No. We can only schedule VA patients once we have an active VA referral on file. Call us and we'll check whether yours has arrived.

What if my referral is delayed or runs out?

Please follow up directly with the VA. Delays, expired referrals and new referrals or approvals for more treatment are handled by the VA, and a call from you is often the quickest way to move them along.

Do I need a new referral for more treatment?

Sometimes. If your treatment plan needs care beyond your current referral, the VA has to approve it first. We'll let you know, and you may need to contact the VA to request it.
