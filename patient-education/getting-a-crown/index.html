Restoring a tooth

Getting a Crown

How a custom crown protects a cracked or weakened tooth.

Book online Find an office

Press play with sound for the narration

What the video covers

  1. Here's what to expect when you get a dental crown.
  2. A crown is a custom cap for a tooth that's cracked, worn down, or weakened by a large filling. It protects the tooth, so it can keep doing its job.
  3. First, we'll numb the area so you stay comfortable. You may feel some pressure, but you shouldn't feel any pain.
  4. Next, we gently reshape the tooth, removing a thin layer so the crown has room to fit.
  5. Then we take a quick digital scan, or an impression, to capture the exact shape of your tooth and your bite.
  6. While your permanent crown is being made, a temporary crown keeps the tooth protected. Some offices can even make your crown the same day.
  7. When your new crown is ready, we check the fit, your bite, and the color, then bond it securely in place.
  8. From there, just care for it like any other tooth. Brush twice a day, floss daily, and keep up with your regular checkups.
  9. If you have any questions along the way, just ask. Your dental team is always happy to help.
